Typography is more than just selecting a pretty font. It's about creating a visual hierarchy, establishing rhythm, and ensuring that content is not only readable but engaging. Duda Sans was designed with these principles in mind, offering exceptional legibility across all screen sizes while maintaining the distinctive character that makes a brand memorable. From tiny UI elements to billboard-sized displays, every weight and glyph has been meticulously crafted to perform beautifully.
