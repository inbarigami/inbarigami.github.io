  • Design Systems

    Or: the perfect position doesn't exi-

    To quote English singer-songwriter Ed Sheeran, "I found a love, for me".

    I've gotten into the Design System world a few years back, while being in a different roles. At some points I moved to a role which allowed me to focus more on Design Systems.
    I fell in love with this field and I feel like it allows me to perfectly combine my love for design, research and organization.

  • From Product Design to
    Product Management

    Or: arguing with myself

    I started at my previous workplace as a Product Designer, and was lucky enough to get an opportunity to dive into the world of product management.

    My role combined both - I researched, planned, designed, measured and supervised the development of some of the coolest products around.
