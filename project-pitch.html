    Design System-ing an existing, huge platform

  • I feel like most design system portfolios are all about components, when the challenge is usually the people. Like in every product, the main task is making the people want to use it. The fact that it's internal doesn't make it easier.

    I joined Gong in 2022 as Design System Lead. The existing design system was a Figma file maintained by 16 designers - all at once. And it looked like it. I went through the regular mapping process of research, inventory, comparison, definition and organization, and slowly fixed the existing mess of a file.

    After two months - Pitch was born.
    Fun fact - I used a company offsite to talk about our new design system, and the name was selected by the audience.
